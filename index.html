Delivery information

  • Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days.
  • Free shipping on orders of ₹999 and above. A ₹99 fee applies below that.
  • You can track every order from My Orders in your account.

How do I place an order?

Add items to your bag, open the bag and press Proceed to Checkout. You need to be logged in, then fill in your delivery address, choose a payment method and place the order.

Do I need an account to order?

Yes. Login or Create Account is required before you can place an order. You can still browse, save items to your wishlist and fill your bag without one.

Which payment methods are available?

UPI, credit or debit card and Cash on Delivery.

How does the MYNTRA10 coupon work?

Enter MYNTRA10 in the coupon box at checkout to get 10% off. The minimum order value is ₹500.

When is delivery free?

Delivery is free on orders of ₹999 and above. Smaller orders have a ₹99 delivery fee.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days. You can check your PIN code under the Shipping tab.

How can I track my order?

Open Profile, then My Orders, and press Track Order on any order. You can also enter your order ID under the Shipping tab.

How do I return an item?

Go to the Returns tab, pick your order and item, choose a reason and submit. Returns are accepted within 7 days.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Please contact support from the Contact Us tab with your order ID as soon as possible and we will help.

How do I update my address or phone number?

Open Profile and edit your saved details, then press Save Details. They are filled in automatically at checkout.

I forgot my password. What can I do?

Password reset is not available yet. Please contact support from the Contact Us tab or create a new account with a different email.
